### What changed on the page

- **Corrected:**
  - "AD 1433, the Compacts of Basel" became "1433 to 1436": the Hussites came to Basel in 1433, and the Compacts
    were ratified at Iglau in 1436.
  - The 1417 election of Martin V was removed as an event, since the eighth era now ends with it.
  - "Mehmed II, 1444 to 1446 and 1451 to 1481" became "took Constantinople in 1453": no source read gives his reigns.
  - "Martin V tried to reform the church without the councils taking control" became Schaff's judgment that he
    ignored the reforms, with the Catholic historian Pastor's explanation beside it.
  - "Nicholas V's election marks the start of the Renaissance papacy" (an event) was removed; his building and the
    Vatican library are in his entry.
  - "The attribution [of the Imitation] is debated" became Schaff's own note on the authorship.
- **Dates where the sources disagree:** Savonarola's death, 23 May 1498 (Schaff and the encyclopedia's heading) or 25
  May (one passage of the encyclopedia).
- **Figures:** "his execution in 1498 made him a martyr to some of his followers" and "the devotional images of the
  Eastern churches, which the councils of Florence and Constantinople tried to bring into union with Rome" were
  removed. The Bernardino caption now rests on Schaff.
- **Debates:** "Historians debate ... The page does not yet give a view" became what Schaff and the encyclopedia say,
  attributed.
- **Added from the sources:** Basel's deposition of Eugenius and Felix V; the Greek rejection of 1472; Lipan; Murad II
  at Varna; the indulgence bull of 1506; Albrecht of Mainz and the Fuggers; Groote and Bernardino.

### What changed on the map

- London, Paris, Venice, Naples, Belgrade and Moscow, which the page does not mention, were removed. Wittenberg was
  added. "The edge of Muscovy" and "Boundaries are simplified" were removed from the intro, and a credit was added.
