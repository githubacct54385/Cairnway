Schaff is a Protestant church history, openly hostile to Unam Sanctam and to Boniface; the Catholic Encyclopedia is a

writings of Aquinas, Wyclif and Hus were not read.

### What changed on the page

- **Corrected:**
  - "kills perhaps a third of Europe" became "a large part of Europe's people". No source read gives a third; Schaff
    says the figures differ, and gives three-fifths for Florence and an estimated half for England.
  - Boniface VIII "issued Unam sanctam ... and then clashed with Philip IV": the bull was issued against Philip, in
    the middle of the quarrel.
  - Wyclif's "views were condemned in 1410 at Oxford" was removed; no source read gives it. The page now gives the
    Constance decree of 1415 and the exhumation of his bones in 1428 or 1429.
  - Michael VIII "the Byzantine general" became Michael Palaeologus, who drove out Baldwin II.
  - The figure caption's "Small devotional diptychs like this were used for private prayer" became the leaf's size from
    the museum record.
- **Dates where the sources disagree, given both ways:** Wyclif's death (29 or 31 December 1384), his rectory at
  Lutterworth (1373 or 1374), his exhumation (1428 or 1429), Francis's approval (1209 or 1210).
- **Lateran IV:** the event that opened this era was removed, since the seventh era now covers the council from Schaff
  and Thatcher. Schroeder's translation on the Fordham site, whose copyright status was never recorded, is no longer
  cited.
- **Debates:** "Historians debate ..." and "the page does not yet give a view" became what the sources say, with
  Schaff named: on Unam Sanctam, on the move to Avignon, and on Constance and the decrees.
- **Added from the sources:** Dominic's choice of the Augustinian rule; Aquinas's career; Lyons 1274 and the Greek
  envoys; the French conclave of 1305; Fondi; the depositions at Constance and the decree Frequens; Lazar and Murad at
  Kosovo; the English bishops' measures after the plague.

### What changed on the map

- Venice, not mentioned on the page, was removed. The intro's "the councils of Constance and Basel" (Basel belongs to
  the next era and is not on the map) and "Boundaries are simplified" were replaced.
- Kosovo was moved 10.3 km, from the middle of the plain to the battlefield memorial at Gazimestan.
- A credit for Pleiades and Commons was added.
