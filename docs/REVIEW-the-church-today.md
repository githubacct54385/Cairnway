# Review ledger: The Church Today (AD 1989 to the present)

Every factual claim on the fifteenth-era page, with how far it was checked. Statuses: **F** full text read,
**P** partly, **W** bare date or office from Wikidata only, **G** a general characterization in the page's own voice,
**X** removed or cut back. This is the page's first full ledger. Before this check, only Pew's figures had been read in
the report; the Open Doors figures and the 2025 papal succession rested on news coverage, and everything else was
unchecked. The page has no map.

## AI source check (CHR-20, 2026-11-16)

Tracked in Linear as CHR-20. Claude Opus 5.5, an AI model by Anthropic, checked every citation, quotation, date, name
and figure against a source it could read, and cut back or removed what those sources did not support. All "not yet
checked" labels and the four review flags were removed. This is an AI check, not a review by a person (CHR-21).

The church and survey sites the page cites were added to the environment's allowed hosts for this check. Even so:
gafcon.org redirects to a host that is not allowed; the Anglican Communion's document page for Lambeth 1998 returns no
resolution text; the Church of England's page for Prayers of Love and Faith and the SBC's Guidepost report returned 404 at
the addresses tried; the Joint Declaration was not found on vatican.va; and the full Open Doors report was not found.
The news-based figures and quotations in the draft were not used, because news coverage is not the source itself.
