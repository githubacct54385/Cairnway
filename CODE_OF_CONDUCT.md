# Code of Conduct

This project is for people of many traditions who share an interest in history. Be respectful.

- Discuss sources and evidence, not people.
- Assume good faith, and disagree politely.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination.
- Describe differing views fairly; do not mock any tradition.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules. This is a short placeholder; we may
adopt a fuller standard such as the Contributor Covenant before launch.
