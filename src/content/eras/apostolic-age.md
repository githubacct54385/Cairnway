## Overview

According to the book of Acts, the first Christians were Jewish followers of Jesus who gathered in Jerusalem and then spread the message to other cities. Paul's own letters give a second, earlier view of the movement as it grew beyond the Jewish communities into the wider Roman world.

Our written evidence for this period is limited. Historians draw on Acts, Paul's letters, and a few mentions in Roman and Jewish writers such as Josephus and Tacitus, and they debate how the sources fit together.

## Key beliefs and debates

Early Christians discussed how non-Jewish believers related to Jewish law, a question that both Acts and Paul's letters address. Scholars also debate the dating of events in this period and the authorship of some of the writings, so this site states the disagreement and names the source behind each view.
