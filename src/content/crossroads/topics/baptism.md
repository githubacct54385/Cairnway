title: Baptism
order: 1
question: What is baptism, who receives it, and what does it do?
status: live
relatedEras: [apostolic-age, second-century]
---
Baptism is the rite of washing with water in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. Churches agree that
Jesus commanded it. They differ on who should receive it, how it is administered, and what it does.
