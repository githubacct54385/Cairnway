Christians share bread and wine (or, in the LDS church, bread and water) in memory of Jesus' last meal with his
disciples. The churches also call it Communion, the Eucharist, Holy Communion, the sacrament of the altar, or simply the
sacrament. They agree that Jesus told his followers to do this. They differ on what the bread and wine are, and on
whether the Supper is also a sacrifice.
