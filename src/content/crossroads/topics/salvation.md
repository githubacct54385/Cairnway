Christians agree that salvation comes through Jesus Christ. The churches differ on how a person is made right with God, on
whether God chose who would be saved before they acted, and on whether salvation, once received, can be lost. These were
among the central disputes of the Reformation, and they still divide churches that otherwise share much.
